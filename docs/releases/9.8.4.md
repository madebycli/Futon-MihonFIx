## Futon 9.8.4

### Comix chapter list

For the externally installed Keiyoushi/Mihon Comix extension, chapters with reliable, unique
chapter numbers are shown together in one continuous list. Scanlator chips disappear in that case.
The original chapter IDs, URLs and scanlator labels are preserved. Reader navigation, continue
reading, download indicators and progress use the same complete list.

If duplicate or uncertain numbers exist, the original group views remain available. Futon does
not remove alternative uploads, change extension preferences or modify/fork the extension.
Comix's own “Deduplicate Chapters” setting still selects preferred uploads before returning its
list. Refresh the manga after changing that setting.

### Compatibility

- Preserve headers intentionally selected or removed by Mihon extensions.
- Surface nested interactive and Cloudflare failures so existing resolution actions remain accessible.
- Targeted adaptations from Kototoro 3e86e87 and b2864d65; no whole-platform synchronization.
