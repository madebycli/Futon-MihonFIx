A calmer, more modern main screen: a floating navigation bar, a floating search bar, and tabs that stay exactly where
you left them.

### Highlights

- **Tabs stay loaded.** History, Favourites, Explore and the other sections keep their content and scroll position
  when you switch between them. Switching fades cleanly from one page to the next.
- **Floating navigation bar.** An optional rounded bar that floats above your library, with a soft shadow, steady tab
  widths and a quick selection animation. Show labels for all sections, only the selected one, or beside its icon.
- **Continue reading at a glance.** An optional round button next to the bar shows the cover of the manga you read
  last and opens it with one tap.
- **Floating search bar.** An optional mode where your lists scroll behind the search bar and the Favourites category
  tabs, which float above them as rounded bars.
- **Steadier bars.** The search bar never stops halfway: when you let go it opens fully, and the content stays in
  place while it does. The last item of every list can be scrolled above the navigation bar.

### Make it yours

Settings → Appearance → Main screen: label style, highlight and accent color of the selected section, bar opacity,
floating or docked bar with its height, corner radius and distance from the edge, the round continue button and the
floating search bar. The new options start off, so Clio looks as before until you turn them on. Your previous label
setting is carried over, and backups include all new settings.
