## Clio 10.4

A follow-up to 10.3 for the details screen and downloads.

### Fixed

- **The download button is back.** In 10.3 the small update indicator took the place of the download button in the
  top bar on most phones. The indicator now sits right next to the download button.
- **All download options while the details update.** The download dialog now uses the chapters shown on the details
  screen, so you can pick a branch, the first chapters or the next unread chapters right away instead of waiting for
  a slow source.
- **Downloads start right away.** "Whole manga" with "start now" begins at once, even before any chapters are known;
  the download picks up the chapters on its own.

Everything from 10.0 to 10.3 is included.
