## Clio 10.1

A quick follow-up to 10.0 for extensions.

### Fixed

- **Opening a manga no longer waits behind the background check.** Some extensions read chapter lists through a
  built-in web page, one title at a time, which can take a minute or more for a long list. When the check for new
  chapters was running, a manga you opened could stay on its loading indicator for minutes, without description or
  chapters. What you open now always goes first; the check catches up on its next run.
- **The check for new chapters is gentler on extensions.** It goes through the titles of each extension one at a
  time, the way Mihon does. Built-in sources and everything else about the check stay as they were.

Everything from 10.0 is included: floating navigation bar, floating search bar, tabs that stay loaded.
