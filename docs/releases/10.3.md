## Clio 10.3

Details and downloads no longer wait for slow sources.

### Improved

- **What you already know stays on screen.** When you open a manga, its known description and chapters are shown
  right away while they are updated from the source. The new data replaces them seamlessly.
  With extensions, the description and info appear before a long chapter list has finished loading.
- **A quieter loading indicator.** While details update in the background, a small spinner sits in the top bar next to
  the download button. The pull-to-refresh circle only appears when you pull down yourself, and the large spinner in
  the chapter list only when no chapters are known yet.
- **Downloads start without waiting.** The download dialog opens with the chapters already known instead of waiting
  for the source. When the update arrives, the options are refreshed and your choices are kept. Downloads no longer
  reload the details when the chapters are already known.

Everything from 10.0 to 10.2 is included.
